## Biological prototype of the architecture

The proposed three-channel model with innate valence and circuit competition is not an arbitrary construction. It is a direct engineering projection of the functional organization of the biological brain.

A detailed justification of this architectural isomorphism is presented in the book **"Functional Architecture of the Brain: Three Circuits of Motivation and Behavior."** Based on modern data from neuroanatomy, neurochemistry, and evolutionary biology, it shows that:

- **Vital circuit (V_h)** — projection of the survival and stress-response system (amygdala, hypothalamus, HPA axis).
- **Cognitive circuit (V_e)** — projection of the cognition and prediction system (prefrontal cortex, dopaminergic pathways, hippocampus).
- **Social circuit (V_s)** — projection of the attachment and empathy system (mirror neurons, oxytocin, cingulate cortex).

The book also demonstrates how imbalance among these three circuits leads to major mental disorders (depression, anxiety, insomnia, schizophrenia, personality disorders, neurodegeneration), and how existing therapeutic methods can be reinterpreted within this architectural model. The mechanism of circuit competition is described as WTA arbitration with hysteresis — both in biological (lateral inhibition) and engineering (arbitration block) embodiments.

## Code example

The repository contains a demonstration prototype of a WTA arbiter with hysteresis, implemented in Python. The prototype illustrates the mechanism of competition among the three circuits described in the books and can serve as a starting point for your own experiments.

**Prototype files:**

- `arbiter.py` — implementation of the WTA arbiter with hysteresis: comparison of signals from three circuits, switching threshold Δ, winner retention
- `main.py` — entry point, launch of a demonstration scenario simulating circuit competition

**What the prototype demonstrates:**

- competition of three circuits for access to the motor output;
- Winner-Takes-All mechanism with switching threshold Δ;
- hysteresis effect: the winning circuit retains priority until competitors exceed its signal by Δ;
- influence of threshold modulation on system behavior.

The prototype is educational and not intended for industrial use. It shows that the architecture described in the books can be implemented in code and used as a basis for more complex simulations.
